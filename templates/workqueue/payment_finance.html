How the figures are calculated

Gross payments + refunds / returns / recoveries − actual Stripe fees = net after Stripe fees. Fees never reduce the amount the client paid toward their invoice. A work credit is not another cash refund.

Only this app’s confirmed sandbox invoice payments are included. Figures use Stripe’s transaction dates in Eastern time. Bank payouts, other Stripe business activity, operating expenses and FreshBooks payments are excluded. Gross payments can include tax and deposits; these are not separated here. Net proceeds are not earned revenue, profit or a bank deposit.

{% if finance_config.enabled %}Read-only fee checks are enabled.{% else %}Read-only fee checks are paused.{% endif %} Unknown fees stay unknown; an estimated processing rate is never used.
