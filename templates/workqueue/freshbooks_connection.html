Reconnect to approve project read/write and creating new billing clients. Select your billing business again after reconnecting. Each filing preview shows the exact client, project, and fixed price or hourly rate before approval.

Existing client and project links are reused. An uncertain creation is held for review instead of repeated. No invoices, invitations or payments are sent by project filing.

How billing works

  1. Connect the billing client to an existing FreshBooks client.
  2. Prepare a draft from an accepted fixed-price proposal.
  3. Review tax, due dates, payment options, and the invoice in FreshBooks.
  4. Send it from FreshBooks and refresh its status here.

The website never sends an invoice or charges a card automatically.
