1. Import payout evidence

In FreshBooks, open Settings → Online Payments → Visit Stripe Dashboard. Confirm the account is your FreshBooks-linked account in live mode. Open Balances → View more payouts → Export. Choose a custom date range covering the full month plus one extra day at each end, all statuses, and All columns (22). For September, use August 31–October 1. The extra days protect against UTC/Eastern month boundaries; only the selected payout month is retained. No new API key is needed.

Export scope and business identity are confirmed by the importer because the CSV does not include an account ID. Saved source files are not an automatic live payout feed.
