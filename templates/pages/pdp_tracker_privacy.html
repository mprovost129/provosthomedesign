PDP Subscription Tracker Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 16, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how PDP Subscription Tracker handles information when you use the iOS application.

PDP Subscription Tracker is designed to work without an account, bank connection, server database, advertising service, or cloud synchronization.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

Information you enter into PDP Subscription Tracker is stored locally on your device. This may include:

  • Subscription names and prices
  • Monthly or yearly billing cycles
  • Renewal dates and subscription status
  • Categories and notes
  • Renewal-reminder preferences
  • Currency and application settings

Provost Home Design does not receive or have access to this locally stored subscription information.

3. Information We Collect

PDP Subscription Tracker does not collect or transmit personal information, subscription information, analytics, advertising identifiers, or usage data to Provost Home Design or third parties.

The app does not require access to your contacts, photos, location, camera, microphone, bank accounts, or financial institutions.

4. Renewal Notifications

If you enable renewal reminders, PDP Subscription Tracker requests permission to send local iOS notifications. Reminder information is scheduled and handled on your device.

You can disable reminders within the app or change notification permission through iOS Settings.

5. Data Sharing

PDP Subscription Tracker does not sell, rent, share, or transmit your subscription information to Provost Home Design, advertisers, data brokers, or other third parties.

6. Device Backups

Depending on your device and iOS backup settings, locally stored app data may be included in a device backup managed by Apple. Provost Home Design does not receive or have access to that backup. Apple’s handling of backups is governed by Apple’s own terms and privacy policies.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Subscription information remains on your device until you edit or delete it. You can delete individual subscriptions or use the Clear All Subscription Data control in Settings.

Removing PDP Subscription Tracker from your device also removes its locally stored app data, subject to any device backups managed through your Apple account.

8. Children’s Privacy

PDP Subscription Tracker is not specifically directed to children under 13. The app does not knowingly collect or transmit personal information from children or other users.

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if the app’s features or data practices change. Any revision will be posted on this page with an updated date.
